Climatic Conditions
-------------------
Bukina Faso is located in West Africa and is influenced by the
dynamics of the West African Monsoon system. This system drives
seasonal rainfall, with precipitation levels generally increasing
from the arid Sahelian north to the humid coastal south.
Burkina Faso is located inland and has a semi-arid
to tropical climate, characterized by a long dry season and a
short rainy season from June to September.
In recent decades, Burkina Faso has faced increasing climate variability
and extreme weather events, such as prolonged droughts in
(notably during the 1970s and 1980s).
These trends are consistent with broader regional patterns linked to climate change.
